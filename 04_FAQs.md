## Getting there

### When should I arrive?

The day begins with a welcome at 16:00, so please plan to arrive 10–15 minutes before that. Note that the ceremony takes place later in the evening, after dinner — the full running order is on the [Schedule page]({{ "/schedule/" | relative_url }}).

### Where exactly do I need to go?

All events will take place at Delbrock, Meerssenerweg 1a, 6222 AE Maastricht. A map and directions are available on the [Venue page]({{ "/venue/" | relative_url }}).

### Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is available nearby at P&R Maastricht Noord, which is within easy walking distance of the venue and has EV charging points.

### Where should I stay?

If you're travelling in for the wedding, please mention it in your RSVP email.

We've reserved a block of rooms at a nearby hotel. Details on availability and booking will be shared with guests who indicate they need accommodation.

## Your invitation

### Can I bring a plus-one?

Plus-ones are included where they are named on the invitation. If you're unsure or have special circumstances, feel free to contact us.

### Are children welcome?

We love your children, but this will be an adults-only celebration. We hope this allows everyone — parents included — to relax and enjoy the day fully.

### Can I update my RSVP later?

Yes. If you need to change your response after sending it, just email us again and we'll take care of it.

## On the day

### What is the dress code?

Suits and ties required for men. Women are invited to wear midi or ankle-length dresses, elegant jumpsuits, or formal skirt/pant ensembles. Please no jeans, sneakers, or overly casual wear. See the [What to expect page]({{ "/vibe/" | relative_url }}) for more information.

### Will food and drinks be provided?

Yes. Food and drinks will be provided throughout the celebration. If you have dietary requirements or allergies, please let us know when you RSVP. More details about the menu will be available closer to the wedding date.

### Can I take photos?

We'd love for you to take photos and capture memories. We'll let you know if there are any moments where we'd prefer phones away.

### Is the venue accessible?

Yes. The venue is wheelchair accessible and has accessible restroom facilities. If you have specific accessibility needs, please let us know in advance so we can make arrangements.

### What if the weather is bad?

We're planning for the ceremony to take place mostly indoors, so the weather shouldn't be an obstacle. If anything changes, we'll make sure everyone is informed in good time.

### How late will the celebration go?

We plan to celebrate into the night. End times and any transport information will be shared closer to the day.
